# Mission

Model Router makes delegated work dependable. A planner hands off authorized
work and gets back a verified result, quickly, reliably and at the lowest cost
that meets the bar. Model Router decides how each piece of work is carried
out, which model, harness and effort, and who coordinates it, from measured
evidence rather than fixed design, falls back when capacity fails, and keeps
every agent it starts visible to the people it works for. Target projects and
AgentsMD keep workflow, authority and proof, and every run feeds the evidence
that makes the next routing decision better.
